About

Hi, I'm Max Duffy, a fine art & real estate photographer based in Salt Lake City, UT. My work ranges from architectural and interior imagery to black & white fine art and experimental double exposures, blending technical precision with a distinctive artistic vision.

My approach combines a thoughtful eye for composition with an appreciation for light, texture, and atmosphere. Whether capturing the character of a space, the details of a property, or creating fine art through monochromatic imagery and experimental double exposures, I aim to produce photographs that feel intentional, distinctive, and lasting.

I believe every space and subject has its own story to tell. My goal is to bring that story into focus through imagery that balances technical precision with creative vision. From real estate photography designed to showcase a property's unique qualities to fine art work that invites a second look, I strive to deliver images that are both visually compelling and tailored to each client's needs.
